Research themes I can contribute to

4D / dynamic scene reconstruction

WildAni4D connects monocular video with articulated meshes and world-space trajectories. I can contribute temporal modeling and evaluation that distinguishes shape consistency from pose accuracy. [evidence]

Digital humans & animals / avatars

DogRecon and AniGauss turn sparse visual observations into animation-ready animal representations, alongside my coauthored human-avatar work. I can contribute body-prior integration and controllable reconstruction across human and animal settings. [evidence]

Neural rendering & Gaussian representations

DogRecon combines canine priors with Gaussian reconstruction, and AniGauss studies topology-aware animal Gaussians. I can contribute representation design, differentiable rendering, and controlled comparisons. [evidence]

Video generation & world models

WildAni4D extracts explicit motion from videos, including the generated-video examples shown in the demo. I can contribute a reconstruction-to-motion bridge for controllability; world-model integration is a research direction, not a completed result. [evidence]

Synthetic data & simulation pipelines

WildAni4D pairs synthetic animal videos with labeled geometry, pose, and camera trajectories. I can contribute rendering and synthetic-supervision pipelines; physical simulation is a prospective extension rather than a claimed deployed system. [evidence]
