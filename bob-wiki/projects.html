Projects & evidence

From single-image animal reconstruction to video-based motion: published systems, visible outputs, and my contributions. [publication record]

DogRecon

IJCV 2025; CV4Animals @ CVPR 2024 oral [citation]

What I built: Single-image dog reconstruction with canine-prior-guided novel views and an animatable Gaussian representation. [project]

My contribution: Lead author; developed the reconstruction framework and canine-prior / reliable-sampling research direction.

What it showed: Internet-sourced image-to-3D test: CLIP-Similarity 0.8006. This is a rendered-view semantic-similarity metric, not geometry accuracy. [paper, Table 2]

WildAni4D

CVPR 2026 Findings [citation]

What I built: Video-native recovery of animal mesh, articulated motion and world-space trajectory. [project]

My contribution: Lead author; developed synthetic-video and temporal reconstruction research pipelines.

What it showed: Synthetic test set: MPJPE 91.29 and Shape Consistency 0.0703 (lower is better). These are paper-reported values; units are not specified in the table. [paper, Table 1]

AniGauss

CV4Animals Workshop at CVPR 2026 [citation]

What I built: Topology-aware Gaussians for animatable animal reconstruction from a single in-the-wild image. [project]

My contribution: Lead author; developed the animal reconstruction research line extending beyond the dog-specific setting.

What it showed: The published workshop manuscript reports qualitative comparisons across animal categories; no quantitative performance claim is made here. [paper, Figure 2]
